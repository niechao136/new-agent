fix(filter): compute diversity cap from the final limit, not the retrieval limit

The filter stage over-fetches candidates (up to rerank_max_candidates) so the
LLM judge can promote borderline articles, but the per-source cap was derived
from that larger retrieval limit. A production check for 「科技新闻」 then
returned 5 of 6 articles from a single publisher (ithome) even though the
source_diversity cap is meant to be ceil(limit/3).

The cap is now computed from request.limit, so over-fetching only widens the
candidate pool for the judge without relaxing the diversity guarantee.
